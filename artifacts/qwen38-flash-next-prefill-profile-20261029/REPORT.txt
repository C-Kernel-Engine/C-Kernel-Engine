Qwen3.8 Flash Next prefill investigation, 2026-10-29

Host: Ryzen 9 9950X3D, 16 CKE threads, Q4_K_M GGUF source, 8192-token
generated runtime. Source revision before this change: f4c5f246e3e172255e4df714d332de9ad3bdb0e4.
All numbers below are direct native calls, excluding HTTP, tokenization, and model
initialization. They are observations of one pinned artifact, not family-wide
certification.

Matched CKE/llama.cpp 6089-token input from the preceding speed replay:
CKE prefill 483.0 seconds (12.6 tokens/s), llama.cpp 89.5 seconds
(68.0 tokens/s). Native decode was 6.30 versus 6.12 tokens/s respectively.
See artifacts/qwen38-flash-next-8k-svg-20261029/REPORT.md for the complete
replay settings and limits.

Numerical reset defect:
- The circuit's PLE token and convolution histories were marked call-lifetime
  even though generated operations update them across tokens.
- Before the fix, two identical 2323-token prefills on one loaded runtime,
  separated by ck_model_kv_cache_reset(), produced different full-logit hashes:
  d3d06cb8... then 96ee8170.... The same difference reproduced with one
  thread at 64 tokens, so it was not caused by scheduling among workers.
- The circuit now declares those histories as sequence-lifetime mutable state.
  Generated reset clears their proven arena extents. Extra unbound sequence
  state fails code generation rather than being silently left active.
- The rebuilt candidate produced identical complete 248320-logit byte arrays
  on both 64-token and 2323-token repeats. Its first run matched the original
  first-run hash. This establishes reset repeatability for this artifact;
  independent oracle parity and full recurrent-state comparison remain open.

Performance profile and rejected experiments:
- perf record -F 49 -e cycles:u on a 2323-token run attributed 53.67% of
  sampled process cycles to pack_q4_k_to_packed_meta_x8. This is a cycle
  fraction across worker threads, not a wall-time fraction.
- The hot call is in the routed MoE projection: selected Q4_K experts are
  repacked into scratch on each token. Parallelizing the unrelated prefill
  weight-cache pack preserved logits but did not change end-to-end time:
  109.85 seconds for its uncontended repeat, versus 109.47-110.02 seconds
  for the reset-only candidate. That experiment was removed.
- A direct canonical-Q4_K provider matched the packed provider bit-for-bit
  on varied small shapes, but cost 0.073 ms versus 0.067 ms for pack plus
  projection on a representative 640 x 2560 leaf. Full-model prefill was
  108.46 seconds with the direct provider, a marginal single-run change.
  That experiment was removed too.
- The remaining MoE performance candidate is reuse of a packed expert across
  the rows that route to it within one prefill call. It needs a caller-owned,
  planner-budgeted cache or expert bucketing, exact route-order preservation,
  a memory-capacity fallback, full-logit/KV parity, and matched throughput
  measurements before deployment. The model has 48 layers, 512 routed experts,
  10 experts per token, and 640 x 2560 Q4_K gate/up weights. A blanket
  process-lifetime cache would be far too large.

Qwen family boundary:
- This run tested Qwen3.8 Flash Next only. Dense Qwen3.8 uses different hot
  operations; Qwen3.6 and Qwen3.5 need separately pinned artifacts, provider
  identities, full/chunked numerical comparisons, and perf captures.
- Reusable profiling should report wall time per model phase and selected
  provider as well as perf cycles. Do not infer speed from a kernel name or
  transfer Flash Next's MoE finding to dense models.
